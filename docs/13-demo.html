The Age I Demo

Ten to fifteen minutes. One citizen, a longhouse and a border — to the Feudal Age and the raid it provokes. This page says what is in it, what is scripted, and what is faked, because a demo that hides its seams teaches the next session the wrong things.

How to play it

# the standalone build (Mono backend - needs no Xcode licence). 161 MB, verified to launch and run clean.
tools/u.sh batch Builder.PerformMacBuild
open unity/ClashOfAges/Build/StandaloneOSX/ClashOfAges.app

# or in the Editor:
open -a /Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.0.83f1/Unity.app --args -projectPath ~/clash-of-clans/unity/ClashOfAges
# then: menu  Clash of Ages → Demo - Build scene,  open Assets/Scenes/Demo.unity,  press Play

What is real

SystemState
SimulationReal, headless, tested. Assets/Sim has noEngineReferences; 20 Hz; 10 EditMode tests cover the gather loop, depletion and retargeting, construction, reserved population, scholars and Knowledge, tech gates, territory, attrition, the counter triangle and raid outcomes.
EconomyReal. Numbers are doc 03's, in one Catalog.cs read by sim, UI and placement.
WaterReal, and it moves. One custom shader (COA/Water): shore foam that laps in and out, pale shallows over a deep centre (from the camera depth texture), sun glitter, a 5 cm swell, softened shadows. The bot asserts it animates — and that the land beside it does not. Not built: wakes, ripples, rivers, waterfalls, rain, refraction.
BordersReal — the RoN signature. 1 m ownership grid, radius-weight rule from doc 12, recomputed only when a border source changes. Drawn by a custom shader on a 0.5 m drape over the terrain, eased so a border blooms. You can only build inside your own.
Attrition / regenReal. Raiders lose 1.8 HP/s inside your border; your units heal 1.0 HP/s at home.
The landReal. The Blender village terrain with its baked 4K albedo, plus 19,727 GPU-instanced objects in 128 draw calls. 310 trees are choppable wood nodes and vanish per-entity when felled.
MovementReal pathfinding — Unity NavMesh, buildings carve. DL32: the sim says where, the NavMesh agent walks, the sim is told where the unit actually is.

What is scripted

The opponent is not the AI in doc 06 RaidDirector sends escalating waves from a camp on a timer, and a final wave one minute after you reach the Feudal Age. It does not gather, build, scout or decide. It exists so the demo is a game and so the border and attrition rules have something to act on. The three-layer utility AI is still unbuilt.

What is faked, and how

ThingThe honest version
The citizenRebuilt in session 10, in this repo: chunky, blue tunic, a face; axe / pick / hoe / hammer are separate props he swaps by job, and his hands are empty under a load. His chop is a side swing and every blow lands with its sound. Still true: tools appear and vanish with no draw or stow; everyone has the same face; the soldiers still have the older, thinner bodies.
Unit animationReal skeletons since session 6 (rig_figure.py: 11-bone armature, rigid skinning, Unity Animator; asserted on import). Since session 8 every figure shares one canonical rest pose and one library of 29 clips, chosen by weapon class: citizens chop, mine, farm and forage; spearmen thrust; swordsmen alternate two cuts and block; archers hold an aim; soldiers run at the enemy; everyone flinches; the settlement cheers the new age. Seven of the 29 have no unit to play them yet. The citizen still holds an axe whatever the job. What is still true: the clips are generated from pose functions, not hand-animated, and the bind is rigid because the figures are made of rigid boxes. Historical note — before that: there was no skeleton. lib/figure.py builds each figure as ~30 loose rigid boxes; nothing in ~/blender has an armature, skinning or an action. export_figure.py joins the parts into ten flat limb meshes whose origins are the joint spheres; FigureAnimator assembles the skeleton in Unity and poses it in code. (A nested FBX hierarchy was tried first and arrived broken at the bind pose.) Units are drawn 1.38× life size with a lifted palette so they read at game distance. Every part is rigid, so nothing is lost visually at RTS distance — but it is not a rig, it cannot blend, and it would not survive a close-up. See art/unit_sheet.png.
The Feudal AgeNo Age II building models exist, so buildings do not swap. The advance is the banner, the fanfare, the border bloom, real stat changes, and the sun/fog/ambient shifting to the Age II mood (DL16).
All audioSynthesised at startup from sines, saws and noise. There are no audio files in the repo. It is tuned — impacts land on the animation's impact frame; deaths obey doc 09's voice limit and 120 ms gap — but it is placeholder.
Trees fallingA felled tree vanishes in a burst of leaves and leaves a stump. It does not topple.
Grass colourBlender tints each tuft from Object Info Random; instanced URP/Lit cannot, so tufts share one colour.

The scroll problem, closed without a constant

PROGRESS Q5: the "Windows reports ±120" explanation was refuted, so no normalisation constant could be trusted. RTSCamera now assumes nothing: the smallest non-zero |Δ| it has ever seen is one notch, and every event is measured in notches. A wheel steps ~12% per notch; a device whose deltas vary in size is recognised as trackpad-like and zooms smoothly. Still needs a human hand on a real wheel and a real trackpad to judge the feel — it has only been reasoned about, not felt.

How it is verified

tools/test.sh                      # 10 headless sim tests (batchmode is safe: no pixels)
tools/u.sh play DemoVerify.Full    # a bot plays the whole arc in the Editor and takes 8 screenshots
tools/u.sh gui  DemoSceneBuilder.CaptureLand

The autopilot is the visual regression check: if all eight beats render — land, first chop, first hut, staffed rune hall, border, Feudal Age, raid, end — the whole pipeline from Blender to HUD is intact.
