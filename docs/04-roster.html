Units

The counter triangle — constant across all six ages

The one combat rule to remember Light beats ranged · ranged beats heavy · heavy beats light. Every unit in every age fills one of those three roles, plus siege (beats buildings, loses to everything) and support. A player who learns the triangle in Age I is still correct in Age VI. Do not add a fourth corner.

Calibration target: a swordsman kills another swordsman in 140 / 12 ≈ 12 seconds. That is deliberately slow — it makes reinforcement, positioning and retreat matter, and it gives the animation work in 09 · FX time to be seen. Later ages compress this: a modern rifle squad fight should resolve in 4–5 seconds, which is itself part of how each age feels different.
