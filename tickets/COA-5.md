## Summary
Place the village and hamlets, reviewing **each building's tile**: dry flat ground, off the roads, clear of crossings,
pads that blend into the ground (no terraces), and life around each pad.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Per building: on dry ground (> waterline), flat (slope < 12°), NOT on a road, ≥ 14 m from any crossing.
- [ ] Pads **blend** into the terrain over a wide margin — no hard step/terrace (the B35 pad fix), asserted by sampling the pad edge slope.
- [ ] No building overlaps another or a resource node.
- [ ] Each settlement tile dressed (≥ 3 props / smoke) and reviewed close; passes.
