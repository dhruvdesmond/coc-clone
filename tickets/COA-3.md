# COA-3 · Water — river, lake, sea, coast

**Epic:** COA-TILES · **Status:** TODO · **Depends:** COA-2 · **Tiles:** the water/edge tiles (~24)

## Summary
Place the river, lake, sea and coastline, reviewing **each water tile** so water always meets its banks and the coast /
river line up across every tile seam.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Per water tile: water surface at the sea level, meeting the banks (no floating water plane edge, no gap).
- [ ] **River continuity:** the channel and its water line up across each tile edge to < 1 cm.
- [ ] **Coast continuity:** the shoreline is unbroken across seams.
- [ ] No land tile has stray water; no water tile has land poking through unintentionally.
- [ ] Every water tile has a passing review record.
