⚔️ Clash of Ages

A real-time strategy game for PC. You begin with one citizen and a patch of ground in the Norse iron age. You end with missile silos. Everything in between — gathering, building, population, research, war — is the game. A Rise of Nations clone: its age ladder, its Knowledge economy, and above all its borders — strategy you can see as colour on the ground. Built entirely from procedural Blender assets and Unity.

Engine Unity 6000.0.83f1 Art Blender 5.2.1 LTS, procedural Target Windows + macOS Ages 6 Resources 9 (max 6 on screen) Assets 35 built / ~192 planned Docs 13 Demo playable Nations 6
Read this first PROGRESS.md, next to this file, is the living state of the project — what is done, what is in flight, what is next. It survives lost chat sessions; this hub does not track state, it holds the design. If you are a new session: open PROGRESS.md, then come back here.
